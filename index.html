Our 2025 Milestones & Paris Pilot

Our immediate focus for 2025 is the successful execution of our **Paris Pilot Program**. This pilot involves partnering with 10+ student associations and corporations to provide IndiSanté's services to over 1,000 Indian students and expatriates.

  • Q1 2025: Launch MVP and secure initial pilot partners.
  • Q2 2025: Onboard 500+ users and 20 specialized doctors.
  • Q3 2025: Gather compliance data and publish pilot feedback report.
  • Q4 2025: Secure Swiss partnerships (Innosuisse) and finalize Phase 2 (East Asian) strategy.

Why Switzerland

Switzerland is our next step: a high-trust healthcare ecosystem, world-class digital-health clusters, and a large South Asian diaspora across Geneva, Lausanne, Bern and Zürich. IndiSanté contributes to the Swiss innovation fabric by:

  • Interoperability & compliance

    Designing AI flows that respect GDPR/HDS standards; preparing Swissmedic-aligned documentation; building CNIL/ANS/HAS evidence to port into Swiss contexts.

  • Patient-centric access

    Reducing time-to-care for migrants with multilingual triage and culturally-matched providers in Geneva–Lausanne corridors.

  • Economic value

    Corporate wellness for large employers (IT/consulting) and student health partnerships improves productivity and reduces system friction.

  • Research & training

    Creating anonymized evidence datasets for digital oncology/primary care research in collaboration with Swiss academic partners.

Our Next Leap: From Paris to Swiss Innovation

Company Update - Oct 2025

Our journey began with a clear mission: to dismantle healthcare barriers for migrants in Europe. After validating our model, securing key partnerships (including LOIs from the **Ministry of Health Affairs, India** and **Apollo Foundation**), and successfully launching our MVP in Paris, we are thrilled to announce our next strategic leap: **Switzerland**.

Why Switzerland? It represents the pinnacle of healthcare innovation, regulatory excellence, and a thriving, diverse international community. Our expansion, supported by our application to the **Innosuisse** innovation agency, is a natural next step.

We are not just entering a new market; we are integrating into a world-class ecosystem. Our focus will be on aligning our AI-driven platform with Swiss standards (like Swissmedic and data compliance), partnering with academic institutions for research, and providing high-value corporate wellness solutions to the many international organizations and companies in Geneva, Lausanne, and Zürich.

This move from Paris to Switzerland signifies our evolution from a promising pilot to a scalable, pan-European healthcare solution. We are excited to bring IndiSanté's culturally-sensitive care to a new community and contribute to the rich fabric of Swiss innovation.
